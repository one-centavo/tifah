# Mission

TIFAH is the core system for pharmaceutical product management and traceability, designed to ensure that the supply chain remains secure, efficient, and fully compliant with current regulations.

## What We Build

A comprehensive platform (specialized ERP/WMS) that digitalizes the lifecycle of medications within the distributor, solving the issues of lost traceability, inefficient inventory control, and the risk of regulatory non-compliance.

1. **Inventory & Warehouse Management** — Exhaustive control of stock, locations, and internal movements in real time.
2. **Traceability Engine** — Tracking engine by batch and expiration date to guarantee health and safety compliance.
3. **Distribution & Compliance Logic** — Automated order and dispatch management ensuring that every product reaches its destination with the required legal documentation.

## Who It Is For

- **Pharmaceutical Distributors:** The primary users seeking to optimize their logistical operations and reduce human error.
- **Technical Directors & Pharmacy Managers (_Regentes_):** Professionals responsible for ensuring products comply with health regulations.
- **Regulatory Agencies:** Secondary stakeholders requiring accurate reports and transparent traceability audits.

## Core Principles

- **Data Integrity:** Because public health is involved, batch and expiration date information must be immutable and accurate.
- **Operational Speed:** The system must be agile (Livewire/Tailwind) so that warehouse operations are never delayed by software latency.
- **Regulatory First:** Every feature must be designed with a focus on whether it simplifies or hinders compliance with local and international pharmaceutical standards.
- **Reliability:** The system must be robust and thoroughly tested (PestPHP) to prevent failures during critical dispatch processes.

## What It Is NOT

- **Not a manufacturing software:** We do not manage the production of pharmaceuticals, only their distribution and traceability.
- **Not a retail point-of-sale (B2C POS):** It is not designed for retail pharmacies selling to end patients, but rather for wholesale distribution.
- **Not a medical diagnostic system:** It does not interact with patients or manage medical records.
